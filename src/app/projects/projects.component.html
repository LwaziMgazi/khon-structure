We have been formally appointed by Down Touch Investment (Pty)Ltd as a sub-contractor responsible for supplying essential construction vehicles (plant) for their ongoing project with SANRAL. This collaboration underscores our commitment to supporting vital infrastructure development in South Africa by ensuring availability of reliable equipment. The project commenced in April 2024, and will continue through to 2030.
Khon’Structure successfully completed its involvement in the Provision of Construction Plant for the Widening of Mvunyane River Bridge No. 3808 and Replacement Culvert No. STC 4007 on P258. Working as the sub-contractor for Mqhelewethu Cnice Logistics JV Msalela, Khon;Structure supplied essential construction plant for this project. The project commenced in July 2024.
Khon’Structure is currently engaged in a key project for the Rehabilitation of R415 from Malusi to the Lesotho border. Under a sub-contractor agreement initiated in February 2025, Khon’Structure is supplying essential construction plant to Gemini Industrial Group along with VEA Roads. The project is ongoing and is expected to continue through to 2029. From there it is likely that Khon’Structure will be contracted to provide plant for the tarring of the road.
